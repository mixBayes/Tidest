## What's new

- Open **Usage Statistics** below **How to use** in the menu bar to see today's uses, all-time uses, and a seven-day bar chart. Hover over a day to see its count.
- Counts start with this version and stay on your Mac. Successful window switches, quick layouts, dock additions, and openings from the dock each count once; canceled and failed actions do not count.
- The Window Dock display selector follows your macOS accent color with a lighter glass highlight.
- A more compact statistics window gives today's count a clear focus and puts counting details behind an info button.
- General settings, How to use, About, and permission panels have less unused space; content adapts to the selected language.

## 更新内容

- 菜单栏“使用说明”下新增“使用统计”，可查看今日、累计次数和最近七天的柱状图，移到图上查看每天的次数。
- 次数从本版本开始累计，仅保存在本机。成功切换窗口、完成布局、添加或打开停靠窗口各计一次；取消和失败不计。
- 停靠栏的显示方式按钮改为更淡的系统强调色，跟随 Mac 配色，保留玻璃与切换动画。
- 统计窗口更紧凑，突出今日次数，计数规则收进说明按钮。
- 收紧通用设置、使用说明、关于和权限面板的多余留白，并适应不同语言的内容长度。
